About

I’m Roman Cassini – painter and photographer from West London. My work is about complexity, perspective, quality, and subjective experience. Life is a Weird Dream. Let’s dream together.

Painter & Photographer

I create art that explores the intersection of complexity, perspective, and subjective experience. My pieces often draw from philosophy, science, and surrealism.

My journey spans traditional oil painting, acrylics, and photography, often blending mediums and concepts. I aim to evoke both immediate visual impact and deeper contemplation.

When not in the studio, I’m exploring the ideas behind consciousness, information, and the patterns that shape our reality.
