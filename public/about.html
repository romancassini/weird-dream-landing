About Me

I’m Roman Cassini – painter and photographer from West London. My work is about complexity, perspective, quality, and subjective experience. Life is a Weird Dream. Let’s dream together.

I work across oil painting, acrylics, photography, and mixed media, often blending ideas from philosophy, science, and surrealism. My goal is to create art that’s both visually striking and conceptually rich.

When I’m not creating, I’m exploring the connections between consciousness, time, and the patterns that shape our reality.
